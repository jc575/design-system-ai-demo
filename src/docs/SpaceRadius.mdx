# Space and radius

## Space

Everything sits on a **4px grid**. The primitive number is a multiplier: `--ref-space-4` is 4 × 4px = 16px.

<SpaceScale />

### Why some primitives have no word

Primitives are a big shelf. Semantic tokens are the small set we **allow** people to use. Seven words (`xs` to `3xl`) cover almost everything. Having fewer choices is the point: it is why two designers building two screens end up with the same spacing.

If you reach for something with no word, ask whether it is really a one-off, or whether the system is missing a word. Do not just use the primitive.

### How to choose

<Doc>

| You are spacing... | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Icon and its label, or tight pairs | `--space-xs` (4px) or `--space-sm` (8px) |
| Inside a control, between related items | `--space-sm` to `--space-md` |
| Between form fields, inside cards | `--space-md` (16px) to `--space-lg` (24px) |
| Between sections of a page | `--space-xl` (32px) and up |

</Doc>

Group related things closer than unrelated things. That one habit does more for readability than any specific number.

## Radius

Radius is named for **what is being rounded**, not for how round it is.

<RadiusScale />

- **Control** (6px): things you click or type into.
- **Surface** (12px): things that hold other things.
- **Pill**: fully round, for badges and tags.

Nested rounding rule: an inner radius should be the outer radius minus the gap between them. A card with 12px radius and 8px padding has inner elements at 4px. We do not enforce this in code yet. It is a review check.

## Border widths

Two widths, both tokens: `--border-width-default` (1px) and `--border-width-emphasis` (2px, used for error borders). Focus rings use `--focus-ring-width` and `--focus-ring-offset`.
