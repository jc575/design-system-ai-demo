# Motion

**Layer 2 of 3.** Two durations, both tokens: `--motion-duration-fast` (120ms, state changes) and `--motion-duration-spin` (700ms, loading spinners).

When someone has asked their device to reduce motion, `base.css` shortens all animation and transition to near zero.

<Callout title="Not yet covered">

Motion is intentionally small in v1. Easing curves, enter and exit patterns and choreography would be the next foundation to add.

</Callout>
