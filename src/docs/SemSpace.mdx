# Space

**Layer 2 of 3.** Seven words, `xs` to `3xl`, that cover almost every gap in a layout.

<SpaceWords />

## Why some primitives have no word

Primitives are a big shelf. Semantic tokens are the small set we **allow** people to use. Having fewer choices is the point: it is why two designers building two screens end up with the same spacing.

If you reach for something with no word, ask whether it is really a one-off, or whether the system is missing a word. Do not just use the primitive.

## How to choose

<Doc>

| You are spacing... | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Icon and its label, or tight pairs | `--space-xs` (4px) or `--space-sm` (8px) |
| Inside a control, between related items | `--space-sm` to `--space-md` |
| Between form fields, inside cards | `--space-md` (16px) to `--space-lg` (24px) |
| Between sections of a page | `--space-xl` (32px) and up |

</Doc>

Group related things closer than unrelated things. That one habit does more for readability than any specific number. The grid itself is on [Primitives, Space](?path=/docs/foundations-primitives-space--docs).
