# Empty state

What a page shows when there is nothing to show yet. It is the first thing a new person sees, so it is a teaching moment, not an error.

**Components used:** Button (primary, tertiary), Icon. **Surface tokens:** `--color-bg-raised`, `--color-icon-secondary`, `--radius-surface`, `--space-2xl`, `--type-heading-sm`, `--type-body-md`.

## The rules

1. **Say what belongs here and why it matters**, in one or two plain sentences.
2. **One primary action.** The thing most people came to do. Here the primary comes first. Empty states are centered content, not a footer, so the right aligned rule for action groups does not apply (Decision log, ADR-013).
3. **One quiet helper.** A tertiary link to learn more. Not a second primary.
4. **Do not blame.** "No rules yet" is friendlier than "No results found."
5. **Different empties need different words.** "Nothing here yet" (first use), "No matches for your filters" (with a way to clear them) and "Nothing needs attention" (a good thing) are three states, not one.
6. **The icon is decoration.** It is hidden from screen readers. The heading carries the meaning.

## Edge cases to design for

- **First use** versus **filtered to nothing** versus **cleared everything.** Different copy, different action.
- **No permission.** Say so, and say who can grant it.
- **Load failed.** That is an error state, not an empty state.
